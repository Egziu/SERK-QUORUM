# Product vision

SERK QUORUM is a vendor-independent organizational intelligence workspace. It turns scattered AI conversations into structured, persistent, verifiable organizational knowledge — projects, documents and decision records owned by people and informed, never decided, by AI.

## Differentiators
1. Model independence — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini and xAI behind one consented interface; customers may use their own provider accounts.
2. Confidentiality — strict tenant isolation, minimal storage of prompts, explicit consent before data leaves.
3. Human-owned decisions — decision records with accountable decision makers, frozen content under review, explicit confirmation.
4. Persistent project knowledge — versioned documents and decision history.
5. Independent comparison (Council, planned) — side-by-side review without treating model agreement as truth.
6. Evidence-backed recommendations — no invented citations; AI-assisted content labelled.
7. Transparent expenditure — measured usage ledger; estimates clearly labelled when introduced.
8. Professional documentation and governance.
9. Usability — calm, accessible, fast.

## Customers
Software engineering firms, architecture and construction, research institutions and universities, financial organizations, enterprise management, government, independent professionals.

## Product areas and status
Home, Command, Projects, Studio and Decisions are implemented in v0.2. Council, Memory, Vault, Analytics, Governance and Billing are planned; see RELEASE_READINESS.md. The original commercial charter from v0.1 is kept in PRODUCT_CHARTER_v0.1.md; pricing remains unvalidated.

## Boundaries
No shell execution, autonomous agents, agent chains, external target testing, exploit generation, unrestricted tools or automatic production changes. Independent of other SERK products.
